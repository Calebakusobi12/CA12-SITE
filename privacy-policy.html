Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This page explains how Caleb Production Technologies, using the CA12 brand, intends to handle information visitors choose to provide through this website. Review and update this policy if your actual practices or website tools change.

Information you provide

If you contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, we may receive your name, contact details and the information you include in your message. If you use the meatpie booking form, the details you enter are assembled into a message and sent to WhatsApp when you submit the form. The website form itself does not process card payments or automatically confirm an order.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss orders, provide requested services, coordinate fulfilment, maintain business records and address customer support issues. We do not ask visitors to submit sensitive personal information through general enquiries.

Third-party services

Links to WhatsApp, social media platforms and other third-party websites are governed by those providers' own privacy terms. When you follow an external link, the third party may collect information under its policies. This website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages; the hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the website.

Advertising and cookies

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled in the future, those services may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalise ads as permitted by their settings and applicable law. This policy should be updated to accurately describe the advertising tools and consent mechanisms actually deployed. Do not assume that advertising is active merely because it is mentioned here.

Retention and security

We aim to keep business messages and records only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, operational needs or legal obligations. No website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure; avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through public or third-party channels.

Your choices and enquiries

You may contact us to ask about information you have submitted or request correction where appropriate. We may need to retain some records for legitimate business or legal purposes.
